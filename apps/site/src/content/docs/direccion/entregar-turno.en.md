## What must survive a change of person

A useful handover is not a list of everything that happened. It preserves risks, pending decisions, ownership and the next check. Mar de Fondo gathers BL-042, four arrivals without a security deposit and two missing number plates for the Delta Group.

## Demo flow

**Gather → Review → Prepare → Acknowledge**

1. Gather signals from the Centre and operational modules.
2. Review the summary, correcting priority and removing noise.
3. Prepare the handover. It has not yet been delivered.
4. Simulate acknowledgement by afternoon lead Jordi Serra.
5. Confirm that the Centre no longer shows a pending handover.

The tool may gather facts and propose a summary. One person remains responsible for its content and the next confirms understanding. Acknowledgement does not mean the work is resolved.

Production would require identity, permission, timestamps, history and offline recovery.
