## The decision

Opening the day is not about scanning dozens of metrics. It is about finding what could compromise an arrival, a guest or the next handover. The **Operations centre** brings together arrivals, departures, unit preparation, blocks, open balances, team workload and a seven-day horizon.

## Recommended sequence

1. Confirm the date and scenario size. Mar de Fondo contains 300 units and is fixed on 7 August 2026.
2. Read alerts affecting an arrival today first.
3. Compare pending preparation with blocked inventory.
4. Check whether a handover is awaiting acknowledgement.
5. Open the responsible module; the Centre summarises but does not make the decision.

## What the vision demonstrates

Housekeeping, incident and handover flows share state. Validating BL-018 reduces preparation alerts. Blocking BL-042 increases affected inventory. Preparing an alternative or acknowledging the shift changes the Centre's risks.

> This is a local interactive vision. It does not query production or modify real inventory and must not be used to operate a campsite.

The day is open when every critical alert has an owner, a next action and a review time.
