## From finding to decision

Housekeeping finds that BL-042's air conditioning cannot maintain temperature and an arrival is due at 17:30. The goal is not merely closing a ticket; it is protecting the arrival without hiding operational cost.

## Demo flow

**Report → Assess impact → Block in demo → Prepare alternative**

1. Report unit, asset and symptom.
2. Assess urgency, safety, estimated duration and next booking.
3. Simulate the block. The Centre moves from one to two affected units.
4. Review MH-018 and prepare a reassignment.
5. Stop before changing the booking or contacting the guest.

## Human control

Blocking inventory, moving a booking, accepting a category difference or sending a message are sensitive actions. Production would need permissions, audit, concurrency protection, failure notification and explicit reversal.

Validate who can declare a unit unsellable, which faults involve safety, how compatible alternatives are calculated and who approves compensation. The vision executes none of these actions.
